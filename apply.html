First things first

We're a people business that happens to use a camera.

Our photographers spend their days in clients' homes, working with real estate agents and homeowners. How you make people feel matters more to us than any photography experience. The camera part is learnable, and we'll train you on it. Being warm, dependable and easy to be around is what we're looking for.

If you've worked in hospitality, serving, bartending, retail, coffee or sales, you already have the hardest skills for this job. We provide paid training, all the gear and hands-on coaching.

About the job

About us

Knox View Real Estate Media shoots photos, video, drone and 3D tours for real estate agents across East Tennessee, from Knoxville neighborhoods to cabins in the Smokies. Agents trust us to show up on time, communicate clearly and make their listings, and them, look great. We aim to feel like part of the agent's own team, not just another vendor.

What you'll do

  • Photograph homes for real estate listings using our gear and our shooting process
  • Be a friendly, professional face for agents and homeowners on every shoot
  • Hand off your shoots to our editing and quality-control team for consistent, on-brand results
  • Keep shoot days running smoothly and solve small problems as they come up

Who we're looking for

  • Outgoing, positive people who genuinely enjoy meeting new people
  • Punctual and reliable: when you say you'll be there, you're there
  • Coachable and eager to learn
  • Already a photographer? Great, as long as you're happy to learn and shoot our style and workflow

What you need

  • A valid driver's license and reliable transportation
  • To live near Knoxville or Sevier County and be able to drive to shoots across the area
  • Weekday availability, plus some evenings (twilight shoots) and weekends
  • Flexibility for the occasional same-day booking
  • A smartphone and comfort using apps

What you get

  • Paid, hands-on training
  • All camera and lighting gear provided
  • A flexible schedule
  • Room to grow into video, drone and more
  • An inside look at the real estate industry
