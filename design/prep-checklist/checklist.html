Photo prep checklist

Getting ready for your shoot

A simple room-by-room guide for sellers and agents. A little prep goes a long way in the final photos.

Whole home

  • Turn on every light, open the blinds and turn off fans and TVs.
  • Clear away clutter, personal items and valuables.
  • Have pets out or secured, and make sure we can get in.

Kitchen & dining

  • Clear the counters, leaving just a few nice pieces.
  • Empty the sink and put away towels, sponges and soap.
  • Clear the front of the fridge and hide the trash can.

Bathrooms

  • Put away toiletries, razors and shower products.
  • Hang fresh, matching towels and close the toilet lid.
  • Hide the trash can, bath mats and cleaning supplies.

Living areas

  • Fluff the pillows and straighten furniture and rugs.
  • Clear coffee tables of remotes, chargers and mail.
  • Put away pet beds, toys and loose cords.

Bedrooms

  • Make every bed.
  • Clear off nightstands and dressers.
  • Put away clothes, shoes and laundry baskets.

Exterior

  • Move cars out of the driveway and away from the house.
  • Put away trash bins, hoses and yard tools.
  • Mow, sweep the porch and turn on outdoor lights.

Final walk-through: if you wouldn't want it in your listing photos, put it away.
