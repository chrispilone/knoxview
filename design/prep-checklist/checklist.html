Photo prep checklist

Getting ready for your shoot

A simple room-by-room guide for sellers and agents. A little prep goes a long way in the final photos.

Whole home

  • Clean floors, counters, windows and mirrors. Put away clutter and laundry.
  • Turn on every light and lamp. Replace burned-out bulbs with matching tones.
  • Turn off ceiling fans and TVs. Open curtains and set blinds the same way.
  • Close closet doors, cabinets and drawers.
  • Put away valuables and personal paperwork.
  • Plan for pets to be out or secured, and confirm access to the home.

Kitchen & dining

  • Keep counters simple. Put away small appliances, food and extra decor.
  • Empty the sink. Store dishes, soap, sponges and drying racks.
  • Clear the fridge front and top. Hide trash cans and pet bowls.
  • Wipe tables and appliances. Straighten chairs and remove mats and towels.

Bathrooms

  • Put away toiletries, toothbrushes and shower products.
  • Polish mirrors, glass and fixtures. Close toilet lids.
  • Hang fresh, matching towels. Store bath mats, trash cans, plungers and cleaning tools.

Living areas

  • Straighten furniture, pillows and rugs. Keep walkways and views clear.
  • Clear coffee tables and shelves of remotes, chargers and personal items.
  • Put away pet beds, toys and loose cords.

Bedrooms

  • Make every bed and arrange pillows neatly.
  • Clear nightstands, dressers and desks.
  • Put away laundry, shoes, toys and charging cables.

Exterior

  • Move cars out of the driveway and away from the front of the home.
  • Hide trash bins, hoses, tools and toys. Close garage doors.
  • Mow, tidy landscaping and sweep entries, porches and patios.
  • Set out patio furniture and cushions. Uncover and tidy the pool or hot tub.
  • Turn on porch and exterior lights, especially for twilight shoots.

Final walk-through: if you wouldn't want it in your listing photos, put it away.
